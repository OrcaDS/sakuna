# eFOI request: DSWD DROMIC reports 2015–2016

Submit at https://www.foi.gov.ph → "Make a Request" → Agency: Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD).

**Title**
Copies of DSWD DROMIC final/terminal reports on tropical cyclones and southwest monsoon, 2015–2016

**Purpose**
Research and public-interest open data. The figures will be compiled into a free, publicly available historical dataset of disaster impacts by municipality, credited to DSWD DROMIC. No personal information is requested.

**Period covered**
1 January 2015 – 31 December 2016

**Request**
I am requesting electronic copies of the DSWD DROMIC final (terminal) reports, or the latest available report if no terminal report was issued, for each tropical cyclone and southwest monsoon (habagat) event in 2015 and 2016, including but not limited to:

- 2016: Typhoon Butchoy, Typhoon Ferdie, Typhoon Gener, Typhoon Karen, Super Typhoon Lawin, Typhoon Nina, and the tropical depressions Julian and Marce
- 2015: all tropical cyclone and southwest monsoon events reported by DROMIC that year

These reports were previously linked from https://dromic.dswd.gov.ph (the 2016 event posts and the "2014–2016 Archives" page), but the linked Google Drive files are now deleted or private, so they can no longer be accessed by the public.

I only need the statistical tables and annexes (affected families/persons, displaced families/persons inside and outside evacuation centers, damaged houses, and cost of assistance, by region, province and municipality). Situation narratives, action logs, and any personal information may be removed.

Preferred format: the original Word, Excel, or PDF files, sent by email or as a shareable download link. Re-sharing the existing Google Drive folders (2015 and 2016 archive folders) publicly would also fully satisfy this request.

Thank you.
